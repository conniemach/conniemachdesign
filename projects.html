AI-Powered Site Search

I led the design of an AI-driven search experience to improve product discovery for subscription and rental users across Haverdash and The Ensemble.

Team

1 Product Manager and a team of Developers

Platforms

Web (desktop & mobile), Mobile app

Timeline

6 weeks

⊘ The challenge

Users browsing Haverdash (subscription) and The Ensemble (rental) had no fast way to find specific products across large, fast-changing catalogs. Standard keyword search wasn't enough — we needed something that understood intent, not just exact matches, across two sites with different user goals.

✦ Beyond keyword search

Unlike standard keyword search, this used an AI model trained on natural language — letting users search the way they'd actually describe what they wanted, instead of relying on preset filters or exact product-title matches. Because users could now type full, descriptive phrases instead of short keywords, I widened the search bar and used placeholder text like "try 'a dress for a summer wedding'" to invite that kind of input from the start. A user could search "a dress for a summer wedding as a guest," and the AI would return relevant results based on that description alone — not just literal text matches. The model was informed in part by an internal auditing tool I helped build, which taught the AI how our team classified and tagged garments — giving search a more accurate understanding of product attributes to match against. This meant auto-suggestions could surface descriptive, intent-based matches rather than literal text completions. I designed the interface to make that intelligence feel effortless — smart, natural-language results with no setup or configuration required from the user.

Finally, keeping search user driven

To ensure users weren't constrained by the search functionality, I designed it to be flexible and accommodating. As users typed, we populated results and suggestions that were relevant to their queries. We allowed users to auto-complete their searches with relevant suggestions, making it easier for them to find the exact products they were looking for while keeping the experience user-driven and non-intrusive.

✦ Results

Exact performance metrics are under NDA, but the search feature was adopted as the primary discovery method across both Haverdash and The Ensemble following launch, and became a reference point for how the team approached AI-assisted UX on future features.
